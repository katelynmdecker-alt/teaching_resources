Big Idea 3: The Sign-Change Principle

If evaluating sequential integers yields a negative y-value and then a positive y-value, the graph must cross the x-axis between those two points.

At x = 1
f(x) = -4
At x = 2
f(x) = +2
"Since the sign flipped, a root exists between 1 and 2!"
This is the primary method for finding irrational roots without a graphing utility.
