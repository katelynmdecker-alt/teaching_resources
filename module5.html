Solving Polynomial Equations

Finding the exact locations where \( f(x) = 0 \).

🔍 Graphical Approach

Solving graphically means looking for the x-intercepts. For any polynomial \( f(x) \), the solutions to \( f(x) = 0 \) are the points where the graph crosses or touches the horizontal axis.

Equation: x³ - 4x = 0

✏️ Algebraic Approach

We use the Zero Product Property by breaking the polynomial into its factor structure.

Step 1: GCF
x(x² - 4) = 0
Step 2: Difference of Squares
x(x - 2)(x + 2) = 0
Step 3: Solve for x
x = 0, 2, -2
"Algebra confirms what the graph shows—three distinct points of intersection with the ground."

Pro Tip: Factoring by Grouping

When you have 4 terms, try splitting the polynomial in half to find a common binomial factor.

ORIGINAL
x³ - 2x² + 3x - 6
GROUPED
x²(x-2) + 3(x-2)
FINAL
(x² + 3)(x - 2)
