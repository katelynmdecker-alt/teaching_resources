Algebraic Scaffolding

The goal of "Completing the Square" is to find the missing constant that makes a perfect square trinomial.

$$ \text{Target Constant} = \left( \frac{b}{2} \right)^2 $$

🧠 Ancient Math Hack

Babylonian mathematicians used Completing the Square over 4,000 years ago to calculate land areas and farming taxes!

Al-Khwarizmi later literally named his 9th-century book on algebra after this exact "completion" method.

📜

Solving an Equation

Let's solve x² + 6x - 7 = 0 using Completing the Square.

Step 1: Move Constant x² + 6x = 7
Step 2: Add (b/2)² x² + 6x + 9 = 7 + 9
Step 3: Perfect Square (x + 3)² = 16
Step 4: Square Root x + 3 = ±4
Step 5: Solve x = -3 ± 4   ➔   x = 1, x = -7
