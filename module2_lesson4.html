Big Idea 2: The "Four Synonyms"

In Algebra, we use different names for the same thing. Students must treat these terms as interchangeable:

Zeros
Roots
X-Intercepts
Solutions

"Physical meaning: These terms all describe the exact same feature — where the graph crosses the horizontal axis (y = 0)."
