Converting Quadratics

Vertex Form → Standard Form

y = a(x - h)² + k
→
y = ax² + bx + c

The 3-Step Process (PEMDAS)

1
Expand
Use the Box Method to multiply the squared binomial by itself.
2
Distribute
Multiply the a value into your new trinomial.
3
Combine
Add the k value to the constant at the end.

Worked Example: y = 2(x - 3)² + 4

Step 1: Expand (x - 3)²

Square the binomial by multiplying it by itself: (x - 3)(x - 3)

Common Mistake

(x - 3)² is NOT x² + 9. You MUST use the box!

x² - 3x - 3x + 9 → x² - 6x + 9

Step 2: Distribute the 'a' value

Plug your new trinomial back into the equation. Now, distribute the 2 to every piece inside the parenthesis.

y = (2 · x²) - (2 · 6x) + (2 · 9) + 4

y = 2x² - 12x + 18 + 4

Step 3: Combine Like Terms

Add the k value to your new constant.

y = 2x² - 12x + (18 + 4)

y = 2x² - 12x + 22
Check your work: The 'c' value (22) is your y-intercept!
