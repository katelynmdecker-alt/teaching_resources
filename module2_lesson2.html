Lesson 2: Standard vs. Vertex Form

Compare how the same parabola is described by two different forms.

The Mathematical Link

Vertex form y = a(x - h)² + k tells us exactly where the curve turns. Standard form y = ax² + bx + c tells us where it crosses the y-axis.

Standard Form

y = x² - 4x + 3
  • • c = 3: The y-intercept is (0, 3)
  • • Useful for the quadratic formula

Vertex Form

y = (x - 2)² - 1
  • • h=2, k=-1: The vertex is (2, -1)
  • • Directly shows the minimum point

Switching to Standard: The Box Method

To turn Vertex Form into Standard Form, you must expand the squared binomial. Let's look at y = (x - 2)² - 1:

Step 1: Set up the Box for (x - 2)(x - 2)

× x -2
x x² -2x
-2 -2x 4

Step 2: Combine Like Terms

x² - 2x - 2x + 4
➔ x² - 4x + 4

Step 3: Add the "k" value (-1)

x² - 4x + 4 - 1
➔ x² - 4x + 3
