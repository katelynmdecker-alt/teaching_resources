Big Idea 4: Different Lenses for the Same Graph

Standard Form

ax² + bx + c

Exposes the y-intercept (c), or the "starting value".

Vertex Form

a(x-h)² + k

Exposes the turning point (h,k), or the max/min.

Factored Form

a(x-p)(x-q)

Exposes the boundary points (p,q), or the zeros.

"One curve, three perspectives."
